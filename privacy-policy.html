Privacy Policy

Local Line Driver for Android (package com.locallinedriver) · Effective 24 September 2026

Local Line Driver (“the app”) is a train-driving game published by Dion (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. The short version: the app keeps your scores on your own phone, has no accounts or servers of its own, and contacts Google for three things: advertising through Google AdMob, Google Play Games leaderboards when you choose to sign in, and Google Play billing when you buy a line.

1. Information the app stores on your device

The app saves your progress in its private storage on your phone so that it is there the next time you play:

  • Run results: scores, stop grades, ratings, streaks, and the logbook of completed runs.
  • Records and mastery grades per line, and which lines you have unlocked.
  • Daily-run results.
  • Settings, such as the graphics level and whether automatic train control is on.
  • Which leaderboard results have already been posted to Google Play Games, so a run driven offline can be posted later.
  • Perks earned by watching a rewarded ad: a second attempt at the day's daily run, and a one-day pass on a locked line.

This information never leaves your device unless you use one of the Google services described below. The app opts out of Android's cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Information we do not collect

  • The app runs no server of its own. We do not receive your scores, settings, or any other data; the app's internet access is used by the Google services described below.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash reporting of its own. The only tracking is what Google's advertising SDK does, described in section 3, and you can limit it.
  • The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other personal information.
  • The app itself does not read device identifiers; Google's advertising SDK uses the Android advertising ID, as described in section 3.

The Android permissions the app uses are vibrate, for the click of the master controller's notches; Google Play billing, for in-app purchases; and internet, network state and the advertising ID, which Google's advertising SDK needs to request and show ads.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner strip on the title board, the run board, the logbook and in the cab; an occasional full-screen ad after a run; and rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a perk in the game (a penalty waived, a second attempt at the daily run, a day's pass on a locked line, or keeping a streak through a retry). Rewarded ads are never shown unless you tap to watch one.

To request an ad, Google's Mobile Ads SDK sends Google information about your device, such as its model, operating system, language, IP address (from which an approximate location may be inferred), the app's package name, and the Android advertising ID. Google uses this to select and measure ads, and, where you have allowed it, to personalise them. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy; how Google uses information from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting any ad, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from the PRIVACY chip on the title board. Declining leaves you with non-personalised ads or none.
  • Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in Android's settings under Privacy → Ads. With the advertising ID deleted, ads are non-personalised.

We do not receive any of this information ourselves; we see only aggregate reports from Google about ad impressions and earnings.

4. Google Play Games Services (optional)

Each line has a world leaderboard on Google Play Games. Leaderboards are optional: nothing is sent unless you tap RANKS and sign in with your Google Play Games profile. When you do:

  • Google provides the app with your Play Games player ID, display name, and avatar so the app can show that you are signed in.
  • The app sends your best score for a line to Google so it can appear on that line's leaderboard. Only complete, free runs of a whole line are posted.
  • Whether other players can see your entry depends on the visibility of your Play Games profile, which you control in Google Play Games.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out or delete your Play Games profile and its data through the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings; we hold no copy of it.

5. In-app purchases through Google Play

Locked lines can be bought outright through Google Play's billing system. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details. Google tells the app which products your Google account owns so that a purchased line can be unlocked, including after you reinstall the app or change phones. Purchase records are kept by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

7. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone; ads are requested from Google as described in section 3. Google requires an account of a qualifying age to use Play Games leaderboards and to make purchases; those age rules are Google's.

8. Security and retention

Data saved by the app stays in the app's private storage, which other apps on your phone cannot read. It is kept until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Data held by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. Progress on your phone is under your control through Android's app settings. Leaderboard entries and purchase history are under your control through your Google account, and advertising choices through the PRIVACY chip and Android's ad settings. If you believe we hold information about you, or have any question about this policy, contact us and we will respond.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that sends data anywhere new, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
