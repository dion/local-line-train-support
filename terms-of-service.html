1. The app

Local Line is a train-driving game. You drive simulated passenger services, stopping at stations as accurately and as close to the timetable as you can, and the app grades each run. Some lines are open from the start; others are unlocked by earning a mastery grade on the line before them, or by buying them (section 5). Every finished run is scored and reported to our server, and complete runs of a line are ranked on that line's world board (section 3).

3. World boards and board names

Each line has world boards, kept on a server we run, that rank complete, free runs of the whole line for the day, the week, and all time. The app reports every finished run to the server, which rescores it from the per-station results and decides whether it qualifies; a run picked up from a wreck, a daily run, or a run of part of a line does not. The server's score is final where it differs from the one shown in the cab. You need no account: the app identifies itself with a random player ID (see the Privacy Policy).

You may choose a name to show beside your scores. Names must be 3 to 14 letters, digits and spaces. Do not pick a name that is offensive, that impersonates someone else, that includes contact details, or that infringes anyone's rights. We may change or remove a name, remove scores, or bar a player from the boards where we reasonably believe a name breaks these rules or a score was obtained by cheating, tampering, or a bug, without notice and without liability. Board rows for the day and week expire on their own; see the Privacy Policy for retention.

If you turn alerts on, the app may send you a notification when another player beats your best on a line. Alerts are optional and can be switched off at any time with the ALERTS chip. Boards, ranks, names, and alerts are for entertainment, carry no monetary value, and may be reset, changed, or withdrawn in a future version.
